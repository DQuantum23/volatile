What Volatile Tracks

The four signals that
precede meaningful moves.

V

Volume anomalies

Sudden spikes in trading volume — especially on assets that have been quiet — indicate repositioning. Institutions don't announce their moves. Volume does.

S

Sentiment divergence

When social sentiment, news tone, and price start moving in different directions, something is about to give. The gap between narrative and price is where opportunity hides.

C

Correlation breaks

Historical relationships between assets suddenly diverge. When normally correlated instruments decouple, it signals regime shifts and institutional repositioning is happening at scale.

L

Liquidity shifts

Bid-ask spreads widen or narrow unexpectedly. Order book depth changes. These microstructure signals reveal where smart money is positioning before the crowd moves.
